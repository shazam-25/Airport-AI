The project is for the safety violation at Aiport tarmac during the turnaround activity. The project detects 3 types of safety violations:
1. Safety violation near the Airplane during Convex Hull
2. Worker PPE violation (e.g safety vest)
3. Foriegn Object Detection near Airplane or during the turnaround activity
